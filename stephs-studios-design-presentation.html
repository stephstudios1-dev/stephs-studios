Which Direction Speaks to You?

There are no wrong answers — this is all about what feels right for you and the audience you want to attract. Take your time, trust your gut.

Dark Magazine
Editorial · Mint · Modern
02
Luxury Portfolio

03
Warm Beauty
Feminine · Serif · Warm
04
Dramatic Bold
Minimal · Pure Black · Type
05
Creator Agency
Bold · Graphic · Yellow

Things to consider when choosing

  • Who is your target client? Luxury / high-end brands lean toward 02 or 03. Challenger brands and startups suit 05. Agencies and content-first brands may prefer 01 or 04.
  • What's your personal style? Warm, feminine, and approachable? → Design 03. Dark, bold, and editorial? → Designs 01, 04. Polished and graphic? → Design 05.
  • You can mix elements. Once you choose a direction, we can blend colours, fonts, or layouts from other designs.
  • No logo yet? No problem — once you choose a direction, we'll develop a logo to match the brand personality.
  • Feel free to note anything you love or dislike about any design — even small details help!
