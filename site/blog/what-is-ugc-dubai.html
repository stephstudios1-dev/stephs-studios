What Is UGC and Why Every Brand in Dubai Needs It Right Now

By Steph Parkinson  |  May 2026  |  6 min read

Right, so I know you have heard the term. UGC. User Generated Content. Everyone is banging on about it and brands in Dubai are starting to take notice, but there is still a massive chunk of businesses out here that are not quite sure what it actually means for them. So let me break it down properly.

What UGC Actually Is

In the simplest terms, UGC is content that looks like it was made by a real person, not a brand. Think phone-filmed videos, genuine-feeling reviews, unboxings done in someone's living room, talking head clips where someone speaks directly to camera about a product they actually like. It is the antidote to the overly polished, overly scripted ad that every single person on the internet has learned to scroll straight past.

Now, there are two kinds. There is organic UGC, where actual customers post about your brand themselves. And then there is creator UGC, which is where someone like me comes in. I create content that looks and feels organic but is professionally produced for your brand's specific goals. The viewer cannot tell the difference. That is the whole point.

Why Dubai Brands Specifically Need to Pay Attention

Dubai is a saturated market. Fashion, F&B, fitness, beauty, travel, hospitality. Every single category has hundreds of brands competing for the same eyeballs. Traditional advertising is expensive and audiences here are savvy. They know what a polished ad looks like and they are not convinced by it.

What does work in Dubai is authenticity. The population here is incredibly diverse, which means content that feels real and relatable cuts through in a way that glossy production just cannot. A video of a girl walking through Dubai Marina talking honestly about a product she loves is going to outperform a studio shoot nine times out of ten.

The Stats That Actually Matter

  • UGC content generates up to 4x higher click-through rates than brand-produced content
  • 79% of consumers say UGC highly impacts their purchasing decisions
  • UGC-based ads get 50% lower cost per click compared to average display ads
  • Brands that use UGC in their marketing see 29% higher web conversions

These are not small numbers. This is the kind of performance difference that can genuinely change a brand's bottom line.

What Types of UGC Work Best

Talking head videos are probably the single most versatile format. Someone speaking directly to camera, giving their honest take on a product or service. They work for every single industry and they perform brilliantly on TikTok, Instagram Reels and as paid ad creative.

Unboxing content is massive for product-based brands. There is something genuinely compelling about watching someone open something for the first time. The anticipation, the reaction, the honest commentary. It converts.

Street interviews are criminally underused in Dubai. Getting genuine reactions from real people on the street about a brand or product creates social proof that you simply cannot fake. And Dubai's streets are genuinely brilliant for it.

Day-in-the-life and lifestyle integration content works perfectly for brands that want to show how their product fits into someone's actual life. Not a staged shoot, just a real moment that happens to feature your product.

For Dubai brands especially, where the market moves quickly and campaign cycles are fast, having a creator who can turn around high quality, authentic content reliably is genuinely invaluable.
