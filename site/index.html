Why UGC

Why brands are moving away from traditional ads

People scroll past polished, obviously-branded content without a second thought. But when a video feels like it was made by someone who actually uses the product, something shifts. The scroll stops.

UGC bridges the gap between brand messaging and real human experience. It's affordable, versatile, and when it's done well, it outperforms traditional ad creative consistently.

  • Works across paid and organic channels without additional production
  • Builds trust faster than polished brand ads
  • Looks native to the platform, so it doesn't feel like an ad
  • Quick turnaround from brief to delivery
  • Can be used across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Ads and more
